# Theodore Kasyfillah — Portfolio

Welcome to my personal portfolio website. I'm **Theodore Kasyfillah**, someone with an interest in turning user needs, business goals, and technology into useful digital experiences.

Through this website, I share highlighted projects, my product-thinking experience, educational background, skills, and ways to get in touch with me. I built it with a dark iridescent-glass visual style to make it feel personal, while keeping my work easy to explore on desktop and mobile.

## What you'll find here

- My introduction, profile, availability, and key statistics
- My background and education

- A filterable collection of my highlighted projects
- My skills and contact information
